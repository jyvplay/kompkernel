## New code's proof obligation

PROTASIS is a finite-state predictor, not a linguistic oracle. State is ready, last-punctuation, or ordinary. Four specific whitespace characters are skipped when ready; other characters consume the prediction. At a predicted ASCII letter, uppercase source becomes lowercase, lowercase source receives a ~ exception; all other source characters remain literal. The inverse removes exception markers or uppercases unmarked predicted lowercase letters. Induction on source positions preserves the same punctuation/boundary state because case changes cannot alter punctuation. Original ~ refuses. Decoder input cap is50,000 UTF16 units; repaired planner now refuses a folded arm above the cap. This establishes the implemented inverse, not native-model behavior, token reduction or global optimality.
