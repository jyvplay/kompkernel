- Becker et al., *Compressing Suffix Trees by Path Decompositions*, arXiv:2506.14734v1, 17 June 2025. [3](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.14734v1). Fetched first chunk. The page explicitly labels itself an incomplete draft. Its array of suffix pointers is ordered by colexicographic rank of corresponding text prefixes; query algorithms retain random access to the indexed text. Therefore its index-space/query-speed claims are not self-contained text-message compression results. COLEX borrows only the intuition of presenting suffix structure first, not its theorems or data structure. The paper's whole-character colex ordering is not identical to our component reversal.
