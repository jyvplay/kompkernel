This is exceptionally relevant: synthesize executable codecs per fixed data block, execute inverse against that same block, embed code in formats such as WASM. Their construction-time correctness claim is restricted to that exact block and assumes pure functional behavior, termination and a non-adversarial synthesizer (footnote explicitly says so). It does not prove a codec on all future inputs and does not establish native-chat decoding. AVX-512 nibble packing, ratio/throughput and executable decompression are different resources from complete fixed-BPE prompt cost. ## Anthropic: explicit quantifier and machine-model interface

Pinned the X162 inspected revision16a5ce41304fcdaea4e0be7c132e1c361dc26da5; fetched CountedRoute/Defs, Wrap/Defs, ThreeSatRateStatement and ThreeSatStatement. Source provenance/hashes saved. `DecidesTM` quantifies all n and3-CNF formulas with clauses of length≤3 and no repeated clauses; it requires correct Boolean satisfiability output within a bound using `TM2OutputsInTime`. A TM2 step executes a statement until goto/halt, possibly several stack operations, bounded for a fixed finite machine. `InTimeTM(delta)` has ∃machine,constant then ∀inputs. `ETH_TM` is positivity of the infimum of achievable deltas. `SubexpTM` is stronger in quantifier order: ∃ONE machine, then ∀delta>0,∃A,∀inputs.

`EveryMachineCanBeWrapped` ranges over finite-alphabet machines and PolyT pre/post functions; it requires a wrapper with q*(t+|x|+1)^q bound. `CountsCompactKCNFWithinMinBound` imposes width≥1, at least2clauses,≥4occurring variables; pad4 is the proposed bridge to all ordinary inputs. CountedRoute explicitly connects query-machine counted operations to padding-model solvers. Crucially, ThreeSatRateStatement says its construction exhaustively searches for n<2^(2^518), and the constant C is not explicit. Even accepting the claim would yield no practical compression speedup here. X162's final theorem source claims unconditional ETH failure, but this session does NOT verify it: the bridging implementations, dependency axioms, whole replay and mathematical correspondence remain open. Definitions and their stated consequences are not kernel output.
