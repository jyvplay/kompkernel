"This replaces the bundled simdjson with Jsonifier\n(https://github.com/nihilai-collective/Jsonifier) for Node's internal JSON\nparsing.\n\n### What changes\n\n- All internal parses (module loader package.json reads,\n  `node.config.json`, SEA config, snapshot and inspector profile data,\n  `node --run`) go through a new parser-agnostic interface,\n  `src/node_json.h`. Nothing under `src/` includes Jsonifier headers.\n- `deps/simdjson` is removed, together with `--shared-simdjson*`,\n  `update-simdjson.sh`, and its LICENSE, nix and GN entries.\n- `tools/dep_updaters/update-jsonifier.sh` is added, and\n  `process.versions.jsonifier` is exposed.\n- SEA config parse errors now read\n  `Cannot parse JSON from <file>: <code>`; the SEA tests are updated.\n- New tests: `test/parallel/test-package-json-parse.js`. New benchmarks:\n  `benchmark/module/package-json-parse.js` and\n  `benchmark/module/package-json-corpus.js`.\n\n### Performance\n\nMeasured with `benchmark/compare.js` (30 runs, Welch's t-test), old =\nupstream with simdjson 5.1.1, new = this branch. The corpus benchmark\nparses every unique package.json under `deps/`, bucketed by size:\n\n| Platform | 0-512 B | 512-1024 B | 1-2 KB | 2-64 KB |\n|:---|---:|---:|---:|---:|\n| Linux, Clang, AVX-512 (EPYC 9V45) | +14.1% `***` | +26.2% `***` | +11.2% `***` | +6.0% `***` |\n| Linux, GCC, AVX-512 (EPYC 9V45) | +18.2% `***` | +8.9% `***` | +2.5% `*` | +2.5% |\n| Linux, Clang, AVX2 (i9-14900KF) | +4.5% `***` | +12.6% `***` | +9.9% `***` | +8.1% `***` |\n| macOS, Clang, NEON (M2 Pro) | +12.8% `***` | +10.8% `***` | +15.2% `***` | +11.1% `***` |\n| macOS, GCC, NEON (M2 Pro) | +12.9% `***` | +6.9% `**` | +3.9% | +8.2% `***` |\n| Windows, ClangCL, AVX-512 (EPYC 9V45) | +3.5% `**` | **-4.3%** `***` | +3.1% `**` | +21.3% `***` |\n| Windows, ClangCL, AVX2 (i9-14900KF) | +2.9% `**` | +0.5% | +8.2% `***` | +1.5% `**` |\n\nConfidence (Welch's t-test): `*` p < 0.05, `**` p < 0.01, `***` p < 0.001.\nPositive means Jsonifier is faster than simdjson 5.1.1.\n\nThe one regression is Windows 512-1024 B, which I am still\ninvestigating. The Linux GCC AVX2 run was too noisy to report and will\nbe rerun.\n\n### Notes for reviewers\n\n- Distributions that used `--shared-simdjson` lose that option. Happy\n  to add `--shared-jsonifier` if wanted.\n\n<!-- AI disclosure per doc/contributing/ai-guidelines.md -->\nPortions of this change were written with AI assistance (Claude) and\nreviewed by me."
