# Fixture attribution

These are unmodified contiguous excerpts of real technical documents, not
synthetic outlines or renumbered text. Byte ranges, upstream revisions, hashes
and selection rules are in ../receipts/fixture-provenance.json.

- TLS 1.3: Eric Rescorla, RFC 8446 (2018), copyright IETF Trust and persons
  identified as document authors; retrieved from aws/s2n-tls compliance fixtures.
- TLS 1.2: Tim Dierks and Eric Rescorla, RFC 5246 (2008); retrieved from the same
  compliance fixture directory.
- HTTP: Roy Fielding, Mark Nottingham and Julian Reschke, RFC 9110 (2022);
  retrieved from dorkitude/skimmer's benchmark mirror. That mirror lacks front
  matter and contains a partial document; no claim of official-original file
  byte identity is made. Our tested source is the pinned mirrored excerpt.

RFC copyright and usage terms: https://trustee.ietf.org/license-info/ .
The excerpts retain their original whitespace and embedded page headers.
The benchmark explicitly concerns technical-outline excerpts, not complete
RFC compression or a broad independent prose holdout.
