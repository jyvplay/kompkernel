# X175 mechanism portfolio and structural attacks

All measurements below are complete direct-wire prices on the 29 canonical sources, not proof of native-chat behavior. Source-role leaders require the final canonical replay; they are not inferred from raw wins. `portfolio.ts` reuses bounded implementations; only STROFOS is the new repository codec. Sparse lifting is an X174 repair experiment, not a second novelty claim.

| Approach / artifact | Proved or checked portion | Observed direct screen | Remaining gap / falsifier | Structural attacker |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lexical conjugacy, `strofos.ts` | Rotation inverse; exhaustive ternary orbits; original bytes and paid parents | 77 canonical plans, zero raw wins | Native behavior, all-plan parent-price optimum; reject on byte mismatch or paid loss | Periodic words, maximal-run boundaries, tilde and Unicode transport |
| Sparse local integer lifting, `sparse-lifting.ts` | Non-overlap DP optimum for additive isolated-span scores; exact integer inverse | No profitable-span canonical plans; four synthetic controls | Isolated BPE scores do not compose into full-wire scores | Headers dominate sparse occurrences; overlapping attractive spans |
| Whole-run Haar lifting, existing KYMATIA | Exact recursive integer inverse | 202 canonical plans, zero raw wins | Arithmetic structure does not imply token compression | Odd carry, negative detail, field width metadata |
| XOR residual tape, `portfolio.ts` | BigInt inverse and source equality | 27 candidates, zero raw wins | Binary correlation is not decimal BPE locality | Carry-boundary decimal sequences and unrelated fields |
| Ranked Euler walks, `portfolio.ts` | Bounded complete walk enumeration and explicit rank | 24 candidates, zero raw wins | Enumeration cap and rank cost; no global theorem | Two walks with same edge multiset/start; branching explosion |
| BWT, reused `bench/x165/block-sort.ts` | Executable indexed inverse | 29 candidates; six raw wins | Raw wins do not beat strong paid parent; model inverse untested | Primary-index cost, high-entropy text, unfamiliar inverse |
| Anchor distances, existing ICHNOS | Executable inverse per candidate | 23 plans; three raw wins, all same ls source | Niche source wins are not varied gains | Sparse anchors, distance tape and skeleton cost |

The graph enumerator caps 12 edges, 50,000 recursion visits and 512 completed paths; refusal is not a pass. The sparse DP excludes overlapping adjacent number pairs. STROFOS's four-plan threshold search is not greedy per-token optimization and is not globally optimal. None of the controls earns a new name merely by shortening its instruction.

## Universal shortcuts rejected

`modal.py` supplies explicit flags for 27 deceptive claim shapes, covering payload-only pricing, weak baselines, semantic equality, encoder self-report, inverse/native substitution, training leakage, symbol/token transfer, free headers, free runtime, micro/whole-stack substitution, timing contamination, memo leakage, unsupported-as-pass, challenge-as-proof, source-as-kernel, silent repairs, restricted-family universalization, finite-test universalization, character/token substitution, source mismatch, hidden protected regressions, NOT_RUN-as-pass, axiom/certificate substitution, marker ambiguity, false blinding, niche/broad substitution, and inherited repair trust.

The executable logical countermodels additionally show (1) a program inverse can succeed while another decoder fails on the same wire and (2) identical edge multisets and starts can admit two distinct Euler source orders. They do not simulate an LLM or prove a universal impossibility of compression.
