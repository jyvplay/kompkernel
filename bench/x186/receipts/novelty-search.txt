A `find -printf`, `stat`, `ls --full-iso` or `tar -tv` listing carries a WIDE NUMERIC TOKEN on most lines — 24 digits and 6 separators for a nanosecond timestamp — and nothing in the frontier chain collapses it: the residual census on a real 120-line listing shows the final wire is 40% DIGIT tokens. DIASTEMA decomposes the value into its DIGIT MASK (every digit run replaced by # of the same length) and one arbitrary-precision integer, transmits the mask ONCE, and then sends only the SIGNED INTERVAL from the previous participant. The reader keeps a running value, adds the interval, and renders it back into the mask by writing the decimal digits, left-padded with 0 to the total digit count, then filling the # runs left to right. That is exact by construction and needs no calendar, no time zone, no locale and no rounding: 10-digit nanosecond fractions, 4-digit years, 1-digit counters and 19-digit epoch stamps are the same case, and a 30-digit value is as exact as a 6-digit one (BigInt, not a millisecond integer). The envelope is DECIMAL because it was measured: the same interval stream costs 505 o200k tokens as signed decimals, 626 as base-36 and 563 as base-62 — BPE packs digit runs densely and letters sparsely, so the obvious wide alphabet loses. The lines that carry an interval are named by a RUN-LENGTH LINE PATTERN (R120L1), not by a per-line marker glyph, so a 120-line run costs six characters once. Every choice is measured on rendered tokens, and the whole message is gated against the parent: the transform is decoded back to the source BEFORE it is measured, then again AFTER the parent round trip, and the wire plus its clause must be strictly cheaper than the parent on the source, or the codec reports a refusal with both numbers and passes the text through untouched. Honest ancestry: HORA (X13) is the ancestor and is cited as such — it turns a YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS.mmm that follows a same-date timestamp into a millisecond offset, and its own boundary list says what it cannot do (date changes literal, 6- or 9-digit fractions unmatched, timezone suffixes untouched, DAEDALUS parent). DIASTEMA is that idea generalized until the format stops mattering — any mask, any widths, any separators, any position in the line, any digit count — plus the pattern instead of a marker and the decimal envelope, gated against the CURRENT parent (OURA). Measured this turn on real captured material: a 120-line `find -printf` listing goes 1279 -> 1140 one-chat tokens (-10.9%) and fires, while `ls --full-iso` (447 vs 367) and an application log (unfavourable) are REFUSED with both numbers printed.
