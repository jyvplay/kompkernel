# Mechanism portfolio and adversarial interfaces

`portfolio.ts` and `receipts/portfolio.json` are executed bounded artifacts, not independent agents. They return candidate prices, actual inverses or concrete countermodels. Existing mechanisms are controls and do not satisfy the new-codec novelty requirement.

| Branch | Mechanism/artifact | Established portion | Gap and targeted falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency BBWT | Histogram-induced order, six width/order plans | Independent LF byte inverse on tested sources | Paid-parent/native gains unknown; attack ties, empty fields, periods, block boundaries |
| KYKLOGRAF control | Lyndon factors and omega-sorted rotations | Tested fixed-order inverse | Not new; finite-lex rotation substitution destroys inverse |
| STROFOS control | Cyclic row grouping with occurrence order | Existing exact plans and prior paid losses | Permutation price; repeated/empty row adversary |
| Sparse lifting | Sparse numeric differences and skeleton | Executed eligible plans; no canonical eligibility previously | Zero gaps and padding; no prose justification |
| KYMATIA control | Hierarchical numeric lifting | Actual emitted plan prices | Integer skew, signs and boundaries; not native evidence |
| XOR residuals | Numeric XOR tape plus literal skeleton | Reconstruction assertion for every emitted row | Long/skewed integers, zero padding, literal marker collision; raw price only |
| Ranked Euler walks | Graph multiplicities plus explicit walk rank | Bounded enumerator verifies selected walk | Exponential ambiguity and rank price; >12 edges or enumeration budget rejects rather than pretends success |
| Ordinary BWT | Rotation permutation plus primary index | `blockUndo` matches original | Index/instruction overhead; ordinary BWT is not index-free BBWT |
| ICHNOS control | Anchor/reference reconstruction | Actual reader round trips | Reference/contract cost and collisions |
| Subsequence cover | Explicit equal-histogram source classes | Distinct strings share cover `ab` | Cover cannot recover order; disambiguation lower bound emitted |

No branch inherits trust from another's test. The portfolio uses canonical lanes, not a claim that every branch has undergone paid whole-stack or native verification. Fresh whole-source heldouts are screened separately. Modal checks reject 27 shortcut shapes, including program-existence→arbitrary-decoder behavior and Euler-walk-existence→unique original order. Those two failures also have executable countermodels, not merely keyword checks. The detector is a finite regression suite, not a universal judge of mathematical arguments.

The frequency variant's source-level justification requires one shared total order at both ends. Histogram preservation supplies it; absolute-Lyndon substitution is deliberately excluded. This answers a concrete inverse-design question but does not establish Pareto superiority or novelty against all 420 inventoried modules.
